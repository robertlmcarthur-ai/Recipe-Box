Recipe Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Recipe Box does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not track you.

What stays on your device

All recipes are included in the app. Favorites you save are stored only on your device. Browsing, searching, switching versions, and copying a shopping list send nothing to us or anyone else.

What is sent to us

The only time the app sends information is when you choose to use the recipe submission form. We then receive the video link, dish name, and note you enter, and your email address if you choose to enter one. We use these only to review and add recipes, and to reply if you asked us to. We do not sell or share them. The form is handled by Formspree (formspree.io), which processes submissions on our behalf.

Device permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or other device data.

Deleting your data

To have a submission deleted, email us at the address below from the address you submitted, or include the video link you sent, and we will remove it. Favorites are removed when you delete the app.

Recipe Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Recipe Box does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not track you.

If you use the recipe submission form, we receive the video link, dish name, note, and any email address you choose to enter. We use these only to review and add recipes, and to reply if you asked us to. We do not sell or share them. The form is handled by Formspree (formspree.io), which processes submissions on our behalf.

All recipes are included in the app. Favorites you save are stored only on your device. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or anyone else.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or other device data.
